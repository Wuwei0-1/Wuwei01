著作Writing

Papers and articles

Free to read on Academia.edu.

  • AI and codes

    Can an AI learn an error-correcting code?

    A small language model is trained on English written in an error-correcting code. It looks as if it learned the code, but it mostly memorized common pieces of text. Even so, it helps repair messages damaged in transmission.

  • AI and DNA

    Storing an AI program in DNA

    A small AI program is written out as a DNA sequence about one-eighth of its usual size, and it keeps almost all of its accuracy.

  • Robotics

    Teaching a robot tai chi

    A computer-simulated humanoid robot learns a traditional tai chi routine while keeping its balance.

  • Testing

    Why a system can't fully check itself

    Checks written from inside a system share its blind spots. The paper explains why a good test needs an outside view.

  • Policy

    AI and democracy

    Recommendations for lawmakers: limit access to dangerous information, protect people from AI manipulation, require financial transparency, update taxes for the AI era, and help the public think critically.

  • Advertising

    Can ads target your personality?

    Companies can sort people by personality fairly well. The evidence that this changes how people act is much weaker.

Jeremy also writes philosophy, including The Ring of Gyges II, a Socratic dialogue written as a play.

About Jeremy

Jeremy Newman is an educator and public speaker, and the head of Dodeca's Agency in Tarzana, California.

He teaches people how artificial intelligence works and what it means for their lives, without the jargon. He also teaches Tai Chi, Qigong, Yoga and meditation, having trained under Sifu Restetta de Jesus and Dr. Carl Totton.

His talks draw on Eastern and Western philosophy as well as his own research and writing on AI.

    AI, philosophy, Tai Chi, Qigong, Yoga, meditation
